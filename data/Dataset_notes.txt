Main dataset: SSI_annual_reports_2016_2025.csv
One row represents one California hospital-age-procedure annual report.
Reporting years: 2016-2025.
Reports: 72,854. Operations: 6,737,972. Reported SSIs: 37,525.
The 78,583 California source rows include 5,729 pairs of non-overlapping 2020 half-year reports aggregated to annual reports.
Blank cells indicate unavailable or inapplicable values. Recorded zeros remain zero.
Identifiers and two-character signal codes should be imported as text.
Prediction fields use earlier reports. The predicted_probability_of_report_signal field is an annual report-level O>E signal probability.
Source-published predicted_ssi_cases is a separate risk-adjusted count.
Time-to-alert tables use reporting years and annual report signals.
National microbiology and AMR summaries are separate populations and are not linked to individual California reports. Their counts are not additive to California totals.
AMR tested-count and resistant-count ranges are rounding-compatible reconstructions, not exact isolate counts.
Figure source tables retain their original variable names, effect estimates, confidence intervals, P values and q values.
SSI-depth composition tables count pathogen reports classified by depth. SSI-event denominators are supplied separately in national_ssi_source_totals.csv.
